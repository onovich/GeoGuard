Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original game art direction concept board, low-poly 3D with stylized cel rendering (3D rendered to look 2D), 16:9 wide.
Primary request: GeoGuard direction 04, chunky matte rounded polygon sculpture. Minimal, elegant, original characterful low-poly 3D game art with crisp three-band toon shading and a very thin dark outline. A few big softened polygon masses, a single broad bevel, faceted silhouettes with approximately 8–12 broad segments on round parts. No high-poly smooth photorealism. Clearly visible top plane and small side planes give volume, not flat vector icons. Shapes should feel restrained matte objects, not shiny plastic or clay photography.
Camera and composition: orthographic near-top-down camera 80 degrees above ground, no isometric 45 degree camera, no depth of field or perspective convergence. Upper 60 percent a real playable free-roaming survival tower defense battle; lower 40 percent neatly spaced orthographic asset studies of PLAYER, RAPID, CANNON, PIERCE with three projectiles, then four distinct bosses COMMANDER, HUNTER, PRISM, TWINS. No cinematic poster. All upper actors small and low-height with nonoverlapping footprints. A wide uniform pale desaturated lavender ground plane, no props or scenery, no obstacles, rocks, roads, tower-network lines or permanent range circles. Single directional light, short consistent hard cast shadows, no long shadows or ambient occlusion dirt. Friendlies muted cobalt and warm white, enemies vermilion and deep navy, resources emerald diamonds. Elegant limited palette.
Rendering: polygonal matte solids, flat cel-shaded patches, maximum three values per material, very fine crisp outline only to aid silhouette. No PBR metal, reflections, glass, translucent crystals, glow, bloom, particles, textures, grain, soft gradients, atmospheric fog or photographic lighting. A distinct round sculptural language using big silhouette and negative space, no small bolts or facial detail.
Battle: a clearly independent warm-white player sensor pod, squat teardrop-kite body with cobalt upper plane and one broad direction notch, NO gun or turret pedestal. Six towers: two round squat lobed rapid turrets with a short single offset nozzle, two square cushion-block cannons with a very wide short muzzle, two triangular low wedge needle turrets with a long-axis barrel. Retain circle/square/triangle category recognition while giving each a designed silhouette. Approximately sixteen ordinary enemies in three contrasting families (fast forward wedge, broad block shield, forked aperture carrier), vermilion upper planes with navy shaded sides. A Commander boss upper right is only three times ordinary enemy body diameter, not oversized, low-height and clearly separate from actors behind it.
Projectiles seen in both fight and studies: RAPID short solid light-cobalt capsule, CANNON larger round body with dark center, PIERCE short tapered needle. Hostile projectiles solid vermilion arrow chips, distinct from emerald diamond pickups. Keep bullets at low constant height with high screen contrast and no motion blur; bullets may use flat unlit material for readability, no bullet cast shadows.
Danger telegraph sits flat on ground: thin hard vermilion border, a few spaced diagonal hatch marks and extremely faint fill, doesn't obscure actors; cast shadows are neutral grey and cannot look like warning areas. Tiny practical HUD "HP 100/100", "WAVE 08", "GEM 240", three compact build cards "RAPID 15", "CANNON 40", "PIERCE 80".
Boss studies retain genuinely different silhouettes:
COMMANDER broad squat arch-shield block with two thick detached shield arms and one horizontal aperture, grounded symmetry and disciplined weight.
HUNTER asymmetrical compressed forward wedge with one elongated leading jaw and one short balancing tail, urgent prowling stance, not a generic arrow.

TWINS two independent complementary bodies, sun a solid polygon disk with three short chunky square lobes, moon a broad solid crescent with an offset aperture, not many cog teeth, friendly-to-each-other yet hostile to player.

Lower studies can be larger than battle but aren't giant scenic illustrations. Labels short clean English, no paragraph or slogan.
Practical constraints: current ordinary entities about 16–36px diameter and boss main bodies about 48–68px in game; keep secondary detailing dispensable. Preserve real arena clarity, large empty movement space, limited vertical height. This deliverable is a concept image, not an actual model or benchmark.
