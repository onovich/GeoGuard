Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original minimalist vector game concept board for GeoGuard, 16:9 wide.
Primary request: highly distinctive, characterful silhouettes using minimal flat vector shapes. Direction 04: eccentric little kinetic machines. The last version was too much like circle/square/triangle UI icons: this version must feel like designed characters and expressive apparatus, while staying extremely simple, elegant and spacious. Use an offset joint, a single oversized working part, a shaped aperture, a clear poised stance, NOT simply a geometric disk with two dot eyes. Original visual identity.
Medium: razor-clean vector-like raster, solid flat fills, consistent dark navy contour of medium weight, at most 2 body colors, 3–4 large components per design. Flat pale lavender-grey backdrop, cobalt/cream friendly apparatus, vermilion/navy hostile apparatus, emerald diamonds reserved for resources. No gradients, shadows, glow, textures, paper, crystal, bevels, debris, scenic props, waves, boats, pirates, bean-shaped humanoids, recognizable existing game characters. Humor from shape and motion potential, not facial clutter or baby toy styling.
Composition: upper 60% an authentic playable orthographic top-down free-roaming battle concept, lower 40% a clean asset study strip. Only short labels, no paragraphs. HUD "HP 100/100", "WAVE 08", "GEM 240", compact building choices "15", "40", "80". Spacious open arena, no roads, tower network lines, permanent range rings. Six towers, roughly sixteen small enemy units, central small independent player, upper-right Commander boss only about three times an ordinary enemy rather than a massive illustration. Danger telegraph with hard vermilion outline, few sparse hatch lines and very faint fill, cannot obscure actors. Small units rather than giant product renders.
Player: cobalt kite-shaped smooth sensor core with one off-center cream aperture and two short balancing nubs, no tower pedestal, no protruding gun, no bean body. Friendly towers retain underlying circle, square and triangle class silhouettes but use distinctive apparatus:
RAPID: a squat offset round spool with a short hinged single nozzle and one thick balancing lobe, one large aperture; looks like a compact snapping turret.
CANNON: a square compressed bellows block holding a very broad circular muzzle in a clean bracket, conveys stored pressure, NOT a square with a stick.
PIERCE: a low triangular tuning-fork apparatus with a split front opening and a long clean needle axis; poised precision, NOT a triangle with dot eyes.
Projectiles: rapid short cobalt capsule, cannon large clean ring with solid core, piercer short tapered needle with one short tail. Hostile projectiles are unmistakable solid vermilion chevrons, diamonds remain emerald pickups. Show examples in arena and studies, avoid blur.
Ordinary enemies share a simple hostile aperture and sharp outer notch, different profiles for fast wedge, square guard and small split-jaw drifter; not static towers or tiny versions of the same face.
Asset strip: PLAYER, RAPID, CANNON, PIERCE with matching projectile samples; four original boss designs in order COMMANDER, HUNTER, PRISM, TWINS. Bosses must differ in mass, negative space and poised personality:
COMMANDER: broad upright staple/shutter body with two thick side shield jaws, one stern wide slit, a single thick core; orderly and weighty, maximum four parts.
HUNTER: asymmetrical spring-jaw pincer with one long forward mandible, one shorter hooked mandible, one offset aperture; compressed prowling posture, can visibly snap forward. Not a generic arrow.
PRISM: tall hovering rhombus shutter with a wide intentional open vertical gap and two separate mirror vanes, one narrow sensor; elegant and composed, no facets or crystal.
TWINS: two separate complementary kinetic bodies, sun a smooth solid disk with three large square nubs and an eccentric aperture, moon a broad caliper crescent with an offset core. Different character postures and opposed apertures, obvious paired relationship, neither copies known mascot characters.
Under one or two boss studies show simple IDLE and WINDUP alternate poses using the same parts, no new armor or particles: Commander shields part, Hunter jaw compresses. Both poses remain minimalist and very readable. Use generous space, clear baseline, studies do not overlap. Four memorable personalities through silhouettes, not excessive eyes or facial marks.
Constraints: prioritize readable shape at normal small game scale, premium independent game art rather than generic interface icon sheet. No claim that image-generated small samples are pixel-exact tests.
